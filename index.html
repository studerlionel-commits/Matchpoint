Scoring

The same in every tournament.

  1. Pick 5 players with 100 credits and give one of them the ×2. It doubles his points and can move before every round.
  2. Before each later round you get one transfer: sell a player (full price back) and buy one who is still in.
  3. The draft opens when the draw is published. Each player locks as soon as his match starts; until then you can still swap him (within your one transfer) or move the ×2, but only to players who haven't played yet. Players you own keep the price you paid.
