Scoring

The same in every tournament.

  1. Pick 5 players with 100 credits and give one of them the ×2. It doubles his points and can move before every round.
  2. Before each later round you get one transfer: sell a player (full price back) and buy one who is still in.
  3. Each round locks when its first match starts. Players you own keep the price you paid.
